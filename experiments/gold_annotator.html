Crown annotator

Open the annotate folder (the one holding items.json and crops/). Enter your name first: progress is saved in this browser under your name and the set, and the download holds everything you drew.

Draw a box around the crown that contains the red dot. The edge of the crown is the edge of the foliage, not its shadow. The tag size is not shown on purpose.
